# Findings

Every number here comes from a committed file in `results/`, regenerated by
`bash scripts/rebuild_all.sh` (seed 0). The pre-declared plans are in `docs/plan-*.md`;
deviations from them are listed at the end.

## Datasets

### Discovery: APObind

| Step | Count |
|---|---|
| APObind pairs | 12,267 |
| After pair-level filters | 7,687 |
| Distinct proteins (holo UniProt) | 1,183 |
| **Labelled proteins** | **916** |
| With the pocket mapped onto the AlphaFold model | 912 |
| Sequence clusters (MMseqs2, 30% identity, 50% coverage) | 671 |

Pair-level filters, in order (each pair counted at the first one it fails): APObind
TM-score < 0.5 (1,957); apo and holo map to different UniProt (1,074); identity < 0.9
(557); holo chains don't map to one UniProt (456); apo has no resolution, i.e. NMR
(438); coverage < 0.9 (98).

Protein-level drops (1,183 → 916): no candidate pair could be labelled, up to 3 tried
(168); no AlphaFold DB model (67, mostly viral polyproteins); model longer than 1,500
residues (32). Of the pairs that failed to label: peptide ligand (290), < 80% of pocket
residues resolved in the apo (25), pocket under 3 residues (1).

Composition: 39% human (361), then *E. coli* (58), mouse (31), rat (23),
*M. tuberculosis* (21) and *P. aeruginosa* (20).

### External: PDB-2019+

40,469 holo entries (released ≥ 2019-01-01) and 16,821 strict-apo entries map to one
UniProt in SIFTS → 1,636 UniProts with both → 971 with a valid pair → **833 labelled**
(84 move > 2 Å). Main drops: too few pocket residues resolved in the apo (85 pairs),
no AlphaFold DB model (68 proteins), peptide ligands (36 pairs). 30% human. 661 are
UniProts absent from the discovery set; 485 have no discovery protein in their 30%
cluster.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Moving: > 1 Å 213 (23.3%); > 1.5 Å 143 (15.6%); **> 2 Å 100 (10.9%)**; > 3 Å 38 (4.1%).

The largest changes are plausible: sirtuin-1 with resveratrol (18.3 Å, see below),

**Sirtuin-1 (5BTR vs 4ZZH), inspected by hand.** Before the ligand fix it was labelled
from a residue of a substrate peptide (a bug, see Deviations). It is now labelled from
resveratrol. The 18.3 Å is real: residue numbering matches exactly, and the deviation
splits by domain. Residues 209–214 of the N-terminal activator-binding domain sit about
28 Å away relative to catalytic-domain residues 291–445 (3–17 Å). Resveratrol binds at
the interface of the two domains, which are arranged differently in the apo structure.

## v0.1: whole-protein features (`scripts/02_train_eval.py`)

5-fold cluster CV, 916 proteins:

| > 2 Å | AUROC |
|---|---|
| Mean pLDDT | 0.528 [0.472, 0.582] |
| Fraction pLDDT < 70 | 0.552 [0.492, 0.609] |
| Logistic, all 23 features | 0.596 [0.538, 0.654] |
| Gradient boosting, all 23 features | 0.574 [0.507, 0.643] |

Boosting minus mean pLDDT: +0.046 [−0.027, +0.124] at 2 Å (**not significant**);
+0.143 [+0.081, +0.206] at 1 Å; +0.068 [+0.000, +0.128] at 1.5 Å.

Which way features point (`results/univariate_auroc.csv`, 2 Å, in-sample): cysteine
fraction 0.39 and polar fraction 0.41 (more → less change); hydrophobic fraction 0.58;
low-confidence pLDDT features and PAE domain features 0.43–0.46. **AlphaFold's
whole-protein "flexibility" signals point slightly the wrong way.** Low-confidence
regions are mostly tails and linkers, not pockets. The PAE inter-domain "hinge"
features, expected to be the strongest, carried no signal. The composition signal is
probably a protein-family proxy.

## v0.4: stress tests (`plan-v0.4-robustness.md`; `scripts/08`–`10`)

Pocket-pLDDT AUROC at 2 Å, discovery / external (`results/robustness/`):

- **R1 shuffled labels:** 0.496 / 0.505.
- **R2 same-size decoy patch > 15 Å from the pocket:** 0.459 / 0.520, against 0.693 /
  0.762 for the real pocket. **The signal is pocket-specific.**
- **R3 pocket at 4 / 6 / 8 Å:** 0.700 / 0.683 / 0.674 and 0.763 / 0.751 / 0.743.
- **R4 labels:** all-heavy-atom RMSD at a matched positive rate 0.691 / 0.727; whole-chain
  superposition 0.690 / 0.761. Spearman with the primary label 0.82–0.94.
- **R5 test–retest** (different holo *and* apo; 186 / 210 proteins): Spearman between
  the two pairs' RMSDs 0.46 / 0.56; 2 Å agreement κ = 0.20 / 0.44. Pocket pLDDT predicts
  the retest label at 0.688 / 0.775, against 0.654 / 0.836 for the first pair's own RMSD.
  The labels are noisy, and the rule tracks the protein about as well as a repeat
  measurement. Small samples (16–26 positives).
- **R6 no ligand (P2Rank 2.5.1 on the AlphaFold model):** P2Rank's top-1 pocket covers
  ≥ 50% of the true pocket in 65% / 64% of proteins (top-3: 77% / 71%). The rule with
  the top-1 pocket: 0.577 / 0.670; with the best top-3 pocket: 0.600 / 0.694.
- **R7 tool checks:** a fresh `pip install` from GitHub works and ships `rule.json`.
  Bad inputs gave tracebacks, and a **PDB entry of the wrong protein silently produced
  a report**. Both fixed and tested.

## v0.6: docking validation (`plan-v0.6-docking.md`; `scripts/14_docking.py`)

A one-off validation experiment, approved by the project owner. The tool itself does
not dock. Each protein's crystal ligand was re-docked with AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 into
three receptors superposed on the holo pocket: the holo crystal chain (control), the
apo crystal chain, and the AlphaFold DB model. Success = top pose within 2 Å of the
crystal pose. Full numbers are in `results/docking/summary.md`.

**Scope (amended before outcomes were examined):** external set only, random order
(seed 0), no new protein after 2.5 hours. 187 proteins were started; the last one, still
running about an hour later, was stopped and is not counted. Of the 186 recorded, 25 had covalent
ligands, 9 ligands were too large or flexible, and 7 failed for technical reasons
(Vina rejected the receptor, the ligand file could not be parsed, or a timeout). That
leaves **145 proteins docked in all three receptors**.

| Receptor | Top-1 success | Any of top 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Holo crystal | 27.6% | 47.6% |
| Apo crystal | 3.4% | 8.3% |
| AlphaFold model | 9.7% | 15.2% |

Re-docking into the holo crystal works less often here than in curated benchmarks.
These ligands include fragments, cofactor-site ligands and pockets at chain
interfaces. Docking into either the apo crystal or the AlphaFold model fails most of
the time, whatever the pocket does.

**Primary endpoint** (AUROC for AlphaFold-model failure, among the 40 proteins where
holo docking succeeded; 30 failures):

| Predictor | AUROC |
|---|---|
| Pocket pLDDT | 0.593 [0.379, 0.793] |
| Whole-protein pLDDT | 0.587 [0.359, 0.809] |
| Measured apo–holo pocket RMSD (oracle) | 0.627 [0.429, 0.813] |

**Verdict: inconclusive.** The pre-declared bar was a lower CI bound above 0.5 and a
result better than whole-protein pLDDT. Neither is met. The sample is too small to
show a difference; even the oracle, the measured pocket movement, is not
distinguishable from chance here. Structure-caused failures (AlphaFold fails, holo
succeeds) were 8.7% in the highest pocket-pLDDT band against 20–25% in the other
bands, and 35.7% for moving pockets against 19.1% for the rest. Those trends point the
expected way but rest on very few proteins. What this run does show is that Vina
re-docking into AlphaFold models fails far more often than into the holo structure,
for most pockets.

## What did not go as expected

- The PAE hinge features contributed nothing.
- Adenylate kinase, the textbook flexible enzyme, is missed ("moderate", pocket pLDDT
  94.7). Confident rigid-domain closures are a blind spot.
- Richer models (29 features, ESM-2) never reliably beat the one-number rule on new
  structures.
- Ligand-free use with P2Rank works but is clearly weaker.
- The docking check could not show that the flag predicts docking failure. It was too
  small, and docking into AlphaFold models failed for most pockets anyway.

## Deviations from the pre-declared plans

1. **Ligand-picker bug fix (after all plans).** Non-standard residues inside polymer
   chains were accepted as ligands: the Fluor-de-Lys substrate peptide in 5BTR, and
   statine and similar residues in peptidomimetic inhibitors. This contradicted the
   documented "non-polymer" definition. It affected 22 discovery labels (3 moving) and
   4 external (0 moving). After the fix (`tests/test_labels.py`), everything was
   rebuilt. External pocket-pLDDT AUROC moved 0.763 → 0.762; the v0.1 boosting gain at
   2 Å moved from +0.073 [+0.002, +0.142] to +0.046 [−0.027, +0.124], so from
   significant to not.
2. AlphaFold-state analysis split by release date: added after the v0.2 plan.
3. Docking (v0.6) was time-boxed to 2.5 hours on the external set, in random order. This
   was decided before any docking outcome was examined (amendment in the plan).
4. E1 was re-run after the overnight attempt failed on a truncated checkpoint download.
   A GPU memory-fragmentation stall on a laptop GPU was then fixed (cache clearing,
   CPU fallback). Neither changes the method.
